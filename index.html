01 — Our story

From one small shop in 2013 to our own building.

Khalil Ahmad began the spare parts journey in 2013 from a small shop, with a simple idea: an embroidery unit shouldn’t have to gamble on spare parts. As the trade grew, he moved to a bigger rented place — and in 2023 built his own multi-floor building dedicated to embroidery machine parts and service.

Today there are three branches — two in Faisalabad and one in Hafizabad — and official dealerships with Hirose, Koban, YZP and Orange Needles. Embroidery units and the technicians who service them come to us because the part is on the shelf and the advice is honest: genuine when a genuine part is the right call, and our own TexPro line when you need dependable at a fair price.

Common questions

Can you tell which part I need from a photo?
Usually, yes. Send a clear photo of the part plus your machine brand and head count on WhatsApp, and we’ll confirm before you travel.
Do you deliver outside Faisalabad and Hafizabad?
Yes — we ship nationwide by courier. Send us the part or model on WhatsApp and we’ll confirm price and delivery time before you pay.
What’s the difference between OEM and TexPro?
OEM parts come from the machine or component maker. TexPro is our own line — spec-checked alternatives at a lower price, for the parts that get replaced often.
